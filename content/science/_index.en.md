      <h3>Cross-Agency Evaluation of Machine Translation Quality in Specific Subject Areas: A Comparative Analysis of Google Translate, Yandex Translate, and DeepL</h3>
      <p class="book-author">Viktor Savostyanov • 2026</p>
    </div>
    <p class="book-review">
      A comparative analysis of the machine translation quality of three leading systems (Google Translate, Yandex Translate, DeepL) in three subject areas (medicine, law, and journalism) using an original cross-agent approach based on YandexGPT. 
      A system consisting of six virtual expert agents and a moderator is proposed. Statistically significant differences between the systems were identified.
